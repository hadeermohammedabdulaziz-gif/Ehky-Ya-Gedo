# 🏺 احكي يا جدو | Ehky Ya Gedo

## 📌 Project Overview

**احكي يا جدو** is an Arabic educational mobile app that makes learning about **Ancient Egyptian history** more engaging through storytelling and interactive content.

The app organizes historical content into **Seasons and Episodes**, where each episode includes a story/video with a description, followed by a short **Quiz** to reinforce what the user has learned.

It also includes an **AI-powered feature** that allows users to take a photo of an archaeological site or monument, identify it, and receive a detailed explanation about its history and significance.

The project aims to make Egyptian heritage **easier, more engaging, and more accessible for different age groups** through a simple and interactive digital experience.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Make learning about Ancient Egyptian history **fun, simple, and engaging**.
- Present historical content through **stories, videos, and visual content**.
- Organize learning into **Seasons and Episodes** for a clear and connected learning journey.
- Add a short **Quiz after each Episode** to help users review what they learned.
- Use **AI to identify archaeological sites from photos** and provide detailed information about their history.
- Make Egyptian heritage easier to explore and understand for **different age groups**.

---

## 📦 Project Scope

### In Scope

- Ancient Egyptian history as the initial content focus.
- **Seasons → Episodes** structure.
- Story-based video content with descriptions.
- Short quizzes after Episodes.
- AI-powered identification of archaeological sites from photos.
- Detailed historical information about identified sites.
- Arabic-first user experience.
